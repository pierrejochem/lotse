# Lotse 0.1.1

A release of the management console and nothing else: the brokers, their formats and the cluster version are as in 0.1.0, so a cluster upgrades by replacing `lotse-console` alone.

## What changed

- **A calmer, more accessible console.** The primary action is navy and red means danger or an error; the focus ring is never red; inputs, selects and switches have borders that meet 3:1 in light and dark ([ADR 0140](https://github.com/pierrejochem/lotse/blob/main/docs/adr/0140-the-console-has-a-navy-primary-action-and-red-only-for-danger.md)).
- **A shell that fits every width.** A sidebar on wide screens, an icon rail with tooltips on tablets, a bar and a menu on phones. A skip link, a page title for every route, and focus on the page heading after you navigate.
- **People and credentials.** Users, role bindings, tokens, certificates and sessions are separate pages with their own addresses, listed in the sidebar. The page names changed: *Tenants and access* is *Tenants*, *Users and access* is *People and credentials*, the message browser is the *Record browser*.
- **Tables that work on a phone.** On a narrow screen each row is a card with its columns named, and the less important ones wait behind *More*. Wider tables scroll in a region you can reach with the keyboard. A selected row shows a bar and the word *Selected*; states differ by shape and word, not by colour alone.
- **Safer confirmations.** Deleting a user, revoking a token, a certificate or sessions, and purging a queue go through one dialog: focus starts on the safe choice, you type the name, and the text says what happens and how to get back. It refuses to delete the only cluster admin. Rows with several actions have a *…* menu; the one solid red button is the confirmation in the dialog.
- **Record browser.** *Oldest*, *Older*, *Newer* and *Latest* page through a partition; the switch *Show uncommitted records* says what Off means; a record's value shows as text or as a hex dump.
- **Overview chart.** The second series is dashed with a square marker, the text alternative gives the range of each series, and *Show data table* lists the samples.
- **Clearer words.** Nodes, Partitions, Requested, units in column headings, error messages that say what to do, and an empty list that says what to do next. The guide and the screenshots follow.
- **Fixes.** The record browser no longer asks for a stream with an empty name before its list has arrived. The footer shows the real version.

## What you get

As in [0.1.0](0.1.0.md): `lotse-console` as a static binary, a tarball, a `.deb`, an `.rpm`, an `.apk` and the image `ghcr.io/pierrejochem/lotse-console`, for x86_64 and aarch64 Linux, built twice and published only when both builds match.

## Not in this release

- A screen reader pass: the console was checked against WCAG 2.2 AA by reading its code and by browser checks, not with NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver.
- Dark mode was checked on the main pages and the dialogs, not on every page.

## Upgrades and compatibility

No change to any format or request. Replace `lotse-console`; the nodes need no restart.
